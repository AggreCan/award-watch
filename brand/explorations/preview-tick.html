Award Watch: the AW as one stroke, ending in a check

The A and the W share a stroke (the A's right leg is the W's first stroke), and the W's last up-stroke carries on past cap height to become a check mark: awarded. Every variant is one path on the 32-unit canvas, filled with currentColor, built from a stroked polyline rather than a typeface, so the letterform is ours. Judge at 16 px first.
