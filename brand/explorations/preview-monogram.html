Award Watch: monogram variations

Every variation is one SVG path filled with currentColor (the two-tone row excepted), shown at 16, 24, 32 and 64 px in both schemes. All typefaces are under the SIL Open Font License. The 16 px cell is the test: a monogram that muddies there is not a favicon.
