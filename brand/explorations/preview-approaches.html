Award Watch: three brand approaches

Three different routes to a mark, not three variants of one. Each is one path on a 32-unit canvas filled with currentColor, so the same file serves the header, the favicon and the dark scheme. Judge at 16 px first.

A. Monogram (typographic)

The initials A and W cut from Source Serif 4 Bold (SIL OFL), as plain letters and as a tile with the letters knocked out of the square. Pairs with the Ledger direction; the typeface follows whichever heading face the chosen direction uses.
