Award Watch: the AW as one stroke, the tail kinking into a check

Second pass. The A's right leg is the W's first stroke; the W's last up-stroke reaches cap height and then bends outward into the tail of a check mark. Every variant is one path filled with currentColor, drawn from a stroked polyline, so the letterform is ours. Judge at 16 px first.
