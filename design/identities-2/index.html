Three directions for the site

Each direction has the same three pages with the same real records: today's page, the open tender list and the profile of WSP Canada Inc., from the CanadaBuys files of 2026-10-31. All three set Inter alone and use white and near-white neutrals, near-black ink, the mark's emerald for open, awarded, links and the check, and one amber for closing within 7 days. Corners are square, borders are 1 px, buttons are flat, and there are no shadows, gradients or images. Every page has a light and a dark scheme (the toggle is shared), restacks its tables below 700 px, and never scrolls sideways. Figures shown as [n] need the open-notices file, which the samples do not include.

Direction A

Sheet

The register as one ruled sheet. A white page, ink and 1 px rules, with no panels or cards anywhere. The mark's own check is the system's awarded glyph, every table ends on a right-aligned Source column, and amounts sit in a column ruled on both sides, as on a statement. Two hairlines make the masthead and nothing in it is filled. The aim is a document a reader trusts on sight, made by people who make good software.

  • The check from the mark marks awarded rows and sits in the Awarded chip
  • A right-aligned Source column closes every table
  • A masthead of two ink hairlines, nothing filled
  • Amounts right-aligned in a column ruled on both sides

Direction B

Console

The product a bid manager opens every morning. A live index down the left counts what changed: open notices, today's new ones, those closing within 7 days, this week's awards, the organizations being watched. White panels sit on a near-white page, and each stat tile carries its change on the line beneath. On wide screens the tender list and the selected notice sit side by side; j and k move through the list.

  • A live index rail with counts, amber for closing within 7 days; a strip of counts below 1000 px
  • A split view from 1200 px: list left, notice right; j / k to move, Enter to open
  • Stat tiles with large tabular figures and a one-line change
  • A dense toolbar with removable filter chips below it

Direction C

Dossier

The organization as a file, and the site as the archive that holds it. A strong type scale, sections numbered like a published report and a 24 px grid under every element (the footer button draws it). Each profile opens with a record line under the name. The home page is a dated issue, Today's sheet, led by the day's new notices. The wordmark is the masthead.

  • A record line under every name, in tracked capitals
  • Today's sheet: a dated issue with a lead table and numbered sections
  • The wordmark as masthead, one line of text links under a hairline
  • Inset tables; the hovered row takes a 2 px emerald rule
