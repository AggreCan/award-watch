How these figures are counted

  1. Non-competitive counts the notices whose procurement method CanadaBuys gives as "Non-competitive" or "Advance contract award notice". Every "Competitive" method counts as competitive. A notice with no method is unstated and sits outside both numbers. The share is shown by count and by value, each with its denominator. Where a limited tendering reason applies, the notice on CanadaBuys gives it.
  2. Award notices count one contract per reference number. Its value is the total contract value on the newest amendment when that is above zero, otherwise the contract amount. A cancelled notice counts zero. 16 of the 24 notices here carry a value of zero (13 of them standing offers or supply arrangements) and are shown as published.
  3. Amounts in a currency other than Canadian dollars are shown with their currency and left out of totals. None of this organization's notices are in another currency.
  4. Years are calendar years of the award date. Each source is totalled by its own rules, and no figure on this page adds two sources together.
  5. Names are merged when they match once case, punctuation and legal suffixes are set aside. The 5 spellings listed above reduce to the same name.
