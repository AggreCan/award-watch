About this list

Every tender notice CanadaBuys lists as open, from its open-notices file, read once a day. A notice leaves the list when it closes, is cancelled or is awarded. Filters and sorting run in your browser.

Region is the region of delivery the notice names. A notice for the National Capital Region appears under both Ontario and Quebec. Closing times are shown as the file gives them, without a time zone, so check the notice on CanadaBuys before bidding.

Each title links to the notice on CanadaBuys, where the solicitation documents, amendments and the contracting officer's contact details are published. This list does not copy contact details.

In this mockup the 25 rows are the notices from the new-notices file of 2026-10-31; the full list reads the open-notices file.

Source: CanadaBuys tender and award notices (Public Services and Procurement Canada), Open Government Licence, Canada.
